01 · What we are building

One sentence in. A scored distribution out.

You describe an event that has not happened. The system finds the historical jumps that rhyme with it, simulates a cloud of forward paths, flattens that cloud into a distribution, and then scores the distribution against what actually happened. The point is never the median. The point is whether the spread was honest.
