04 · The Wolfram mapping

Two of these are earned. One is a hypothesis. One is a category error we are naming first.

Wolfram’s claim is that inside a computationally irreducible process you cannot predict the trajectory but you can still predict the statistics of the ensemble it lives in. We built the market version of that, then spent our time trying to break it. The scene above is the mechanism.
