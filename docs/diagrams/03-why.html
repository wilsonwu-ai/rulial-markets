03 · Why it is built this way

Every guard here is structure, not discipline.

The hardest question anyone can ask a market forecaster is: how do you know you did not just look up the answer? Our reply is that the four guards below are frozen into the interface, so no lane can quietly relax one to make a number look better. The fourth guard exists because the first three cannot reach the model’s weights, and we would rather say that on the results screen than be caught not saying it.
