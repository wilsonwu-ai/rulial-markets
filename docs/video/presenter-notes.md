# Rulial Markets — narration and presenter notes

Film duration: 92.7 seconds. Eight chapters.

## 01 · The question · 0.0–10.0s

Something happens to a company you own. Does it matter? Rulial Markets turns that question into a landscape you can explore.

## 02 · Real history · 10.0–22.4s

Start with real history. Three hundred twenty-nine events. Ten companies. Retrieve comparable shocks, using only price windows that end before the scenario's cutoff.

## 03 · Possible outcomes · 22.4–35.5s

Imagine a Boeing fleet-grounding shock. The engine resamples historical returns into two thousand possible outcomes over five trading days. You see a range, with its evidence attached.

## 04 · The hidden assumption · 35.5–41.2s

But there is a deeper uncertainty. What if the rules inside your model are wrong?

## 05 · The space of rules · 41.2–53.9s

Pull it apart. Change which analogs count, how they are grouped, the drift assumption, and how returns are resampled. Now run one hundred forty-four combinations.

## 06 · Model disagreement · 53.9–68.2s

Same event. Different rules. In this stored example, the simulated fraction of down outcomes ranges from twenty-five to eighty-two percent. A confident-looking answer can depend on an assumption.

## 07 · What survives · 68.2–81.0s

So we ask what survives. Downside tail risk appears in one hundred thirty-seven of the one hundred forty-four models. Directional agreement stays below our ninety-percent threshold.

## 08 · Rulial Markets · 81.0–92.7s

This is a small, hand-chosen map of model risk. Rulial Markets. Explore what could happen. See what survives changing the rules.

## After the film: a 20-second handoff

“The key question is whether our answer survives changing the assumptions. We can inspect each of the 144 rules, see the historical evidence, and separate stable risk signals from rule-dependent direction. This is a working prototype with a deliberately small model grid.”
